## Gold example: long-haul flight-attendant vlog

The example below demonstrates the required three-paragraph logic. It is not copy-ready wording for another video.

**内容价值**

博主真实的空乘身份、机舱后台拍摄权限和完整班次记录能力，为职业内容提供了稀缺性。视频以长途执勤 Vlog 为熟悉主题，通过“小姨第一次飞纽约”的同行关系形成独特视角，让观众同时获得航空幕后知识、亲属互动和高强度职业体验。

**产品认知**

产品被塑造成超长飞行后温和重保湿的落地恢复步骤，核心卖点是“长时间处于干燥环境后，可通过湿敷快速补充舒适感”。长航线、17 小时执勤和卸妆后“脸干干的”共同建立需求；倒入化妆棉、双颊湿敷是主要使用证明，博主关于无刺感、肤感软嫩和近期状态稳定的反馈进一步强化温和、安心的产品认知。

**营销启示**

核心生效机制是用真实职业消耗先证明补水需求，再让产品进入落地卸妆流程；可见湿敷把卖点转成使用证据，主观肤感反馈完成认知收口。视频采用了“职业消耗—需求显现—产品恢复—使用证明—状态反馈”的植入链路。

**潜力话题**

#长途飞行后护肤 #空乘落地恢复

**Why it works**

- Content value begins with observed creator assets instead of a hypothetical KOL recommendation.
- Product perception commits to one perception and one selling point before selecting evidence.
- The visible wet-compress action is named as the strongest RTB; subjective feedback remains attributed.
- Marketing implication explains the principal causal mechanism before naming the chain.
- No performance, media, or budget conclusion is inferred.

## Approved regression examples

Use the three examples below as acceptance references for LIFESTYLE, DRAMA, and SEEDING. Preserve their division of labor across paragraphs; do not copy their nouns into unrelated videos.

### LIFESTYLE: graduation morning

**内容价值**

博主稳定的三代同堂家庭关系、家人自然出镜的配合度和完整记录毕业日准备过程的能力，为家庭成长节点内容提供了真实感。视频以“女儿高中毕业，全家共同准备”为熟悉主题，通过外婆烫头、父亲赶回和母亲统筹妆发形成多代视角，让观众同时获得热闹日常、代际重视和教育情感。

**产品认知**

产品被塑造成中年妈妈在重要场合前快速恢复状态的晨间打底，核心卖点是“睡眠不足又赶时间时，用精简步骤改善疲态并衔接上妆”。五点半起床、还要为全家妆发共同建立需求；掌心倒取、全脸按压和连续素颜近景是主要使用证明，妆后服帖反馈进一步强化快速、适合妆前的产品认知。

**营销启示**

核心生效机制是用毕业日清晨的密集任务证明妈妈“没有完整护肤时间，但必须保持状态”，再将产品嵌入她为家人妆发前的自我准备，使产品同时具备时间与角色理由。视频采用“家庭大事—早起忙碌—疲态显现—快速打底—继续妆发”的植入链路。

**潜力话题**

#毕业季妈妈状态管理 #全家大事里最后照顾自己的人

### DRAMA: sisters and teenage acne

**内容价值**

博主一人分饰姐姐、妹妹和妈妈的表演能力、夸张特效与密集对白，为姐妹题材建立了鲜明的人物辨识度。视频以“嘴硬心软的姐姐”为熟悉母题，通过嘴硬吐槽与实际关心的持续反差，让观众同时获得家庭喜剧、亲情反转和人物关系逐步升温的情绪满足。

**产品认知**

产品被塑造成姐姐为青春期油痘肌妹妹准备的温和日常管理方案，核心卖点是“停止反复清洁，用容易坚持的一步护理管理油痘”。妹妹频繁洗脸仍然长痘、因此拒绝拍照共同建立需求；人参皂苷与七种酸的作用分工是主要理性证明，实际上脸和便携次抛进一步强化适合校园日常坚持的产品认知。

**营销启示**

核心生效机制是用姐姐此前持续送礼和解决问题的行动建立推荐资格，再让产品成为她理解妹妹困扰后给出的具体关怀，既延续人物设定，也完成从情绪安慰到实际方案的剧情推进。视频采用“外貌困扰—姐姐共情—给出方案—机制解释—情绪回扣”的植入链路。

**潜力话题**

#青春期长痘不想拍照 #姐姐帮妹妹护肤

### SEEDING: close-up lazy acne care

**内容价值**

博主愿意用超近距离素颜公开真实肌况，并具备用少量语言和清晰动作讲解护理步骤的能力，为净痘教程提供了可信度。视频以“卸下全妆后的真面目”为熟悉反差，把精致妆面、真实素颜和玩具总动员趣味视觉组合在一起，让观众同时获得真实感、护理方法和轻松观看体验。

**产品认知**

产品被塑造成敏感痘肌无需复杂耐受流程、可全脸调理也可局部点涂的低门槛净痘步骤，核心卖点是“少步骤也能完成日常净痘”。带妆与闷热后出现的黑头痘痘建立需求；全脸铺涂和局部点涂是主要使用证明，数日后的同脸近景与个人反馈进一步强化简单、易坚持的产品认知。

**营销启示**

核心生效机制是先用卸妆反差建立真实问题，再让产品顺接毛孔清理后的护理流程，因此产品出现同时符合问题逻辑和懒人少步骤主题。视频采用“全妆悬念—素颜问题—毛孔清理—双重用法—跨日反馈”的植入链路。

**潜力话题**

#卸妆后的真实皮肤 #懒人痘肌护肤

## 1. SEEDING: persuasion progression

**Pattern:** sensorial demonstration → repeated use → displayed result.

**Example:** The creator moves from sensory recommendation to scientific substantiation by showing a product combination across several times and situations, including travel check-ins, then closes the trial with a displayed wrinkle reading.

**Why it works:** It identifies how the proof becomes stronger over time instead of listing ingredients and application shots.

## 2. SEEDING: topic-to-category bridge

**Pattern:** recognizable cultural topic → category question → product solution.

**Example:** The creator uses the familiar motifs of older-generation skincare and beauty-food crossover to introduce a mature-skin anti-aging routine.

**Why it works:** It explains how the topic gives the product a relevant entry point and target audience.

## 3. LIFESTYLE: familiar motif × distinctive angle

**Pattern:** familiar life-stage format × specific identity or circumstance.

**Example:** Wedding-preparation vlog supplies an immediately recognizable life-stage frame, while a bride preparing for the ceremony with crutches adds a warm, resilient, and specific human angle.

**Why it works:** It separates quick audience comprehension from the feature that makes this particular story memorable.

## 4. DRAMA: audience condition → product identity

**Pattern:** plot event → audience condition → skin-state need → product role.

**Example:** Promotion and marriage are read as high-pressure, high-expectation moments for urban women; the product then takes the role of a routine that protects a steady, prepared appearance.

**Why it works:** It translates literal plot into a Product Marketing-relevant human situation.

## 5. DRAMA: narrative device → viewer payoff

**Pattern:** creative device → tension progression → payoff.

**Example:** One creator plays multiple characters inside an identity-swap premise; exaggerated performance, identity contrast, dense jokes, and reversal create curiosity and comic release.

**Why it works:** It names the creative devices that generate viewing value rather than merely retelling the plot.

## 6. Node marketing: occasion → product job → observed mechanism

**Pattern:** calendar node → relationship emotion → product job → proof.

**Example:** A Mother's Day story turns a skincare set into a daughter's concrete care action; the established family relationship makes the gift relevant, and visible use turns care into product proof.

**Why it works:** It explains why the product belongs in this story and which evidence completes persuasion, without proposing a future KOL plan.

## Anti-patterns

### Plot inventory instead of content value

Avoid listing every scene, joke, prop, role, and timestamp. Keep only the creator asset, motif, distinctive angle, main device, and viewer payoff needed to explain why the content works.

Starting with `视频用`, `视频把`, or `视频以` often signals that the paragraph is describing the artifact rather than judging the creator-specific viewing value. Prefer the demonstrated creator asset as the grammatical subject.

### Selling-point inventory instead of product perception

Avoid giving equal weight to every ingredient, texture, fragrance, claim, and action. State one core product perception and one core selling point, then select the strongest RTB and only necessary support.

### Chain label without causal explanation

`采用了“需求—产品—反馈”的链路` is incomplete on its own. Explain which mechanism makes the product relevant and how the selected proof closes persuasion.

Do not reverse this order by placing the full chain first and appending `之所以成立，是……`. State the causal judgment first; use the chain only as the final compressed summary.

### Future plan instead of video deconstruction

Avoid `适合布局`, `适合与某类 KOL 合作`, `由某类 KOL 承载`, `建议复用`, budget recommendations, or unsupported performance conclusions. Describe what the current creator and video actually do.
