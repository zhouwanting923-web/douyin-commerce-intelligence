# Douyin video tagging rules

Apply the rules in this exact primary-type priority:

`DRAMA -> SEEDING -> LIFESTYLE -> BRANDING -> OFFER -> OTHERS`

Within the same primary type, evaluate secondary types from top to bottom. Return one primary and one secondary type. Record evidence and the decisive exclusion rule.

## DRAMA

1. **剧情演绎/短剧** - Character performance, plot progression, conflict, or reversal is the main viewing value. Positive signals include roles and plot, “虚拟演绎，仅供娱乐”, “剧情”, or “一人分饰多角”. If drama is only an opening hook and the remainder mainly explains a product, classify as SEEDING.
2. **连续剧集/IP人物** - A fixed character, persona, continuing plot, episode relationship, or IP setting is essential. A spokesperson appearing without plot belongs to BRANDING or SEEDING.

## SEEDING

1. **知识科普/搜索答疑** - Provides decision information about ingredients, efficacy, mechanisms, suitable users, selection, or consumer questions. Brand technology or brand history as the main point belongs to BRANDING.
2. **使用教程/方法演示** - Teaches clear steps, routine, technique, or operation with the product. A routine with no teaching value belongs to LIFESTYLE.
3. **产品推荐/好物合集** - Introduces or recommends a single item, set, comparison list, or collection; purchase reasons are the main line. Natural exposure belongs to LIFESTYLE; price/discount/CTA as the main line belongs to OFFER.
4. **测评/真实体验** - Uses testing, comparison, unboxing, first use, empty bottle, follow-up, or usage records as evidence. Subjective praise without experience evidence is not sufficient.
5. **效果展示/前后改造** - Clear before/after change in makeup, skin feel, appearance, or state, with explicit product linkage. A life-change story with weak product linkage belongs to LIFESTYLE.
6. **场景化/沉浸式种草** - Vlog, ASMR, texture, or lifestyle presentation where product experience, selling points, or sensory display remains the main line. If removing the product leaves the content substantially intact, classify as LIFESTYLE; a pure mood film belongs to BRANDING.

## LIFESTYLE

1. **日常Vlog/生活记录** - Records a day, family, commute, GRWM, or everyday process where the product is only one component. If product explanation, testing, recommendation, or teaching is the main line, classify as SEEDING.
2. **生活方式/场景内容** - Persona, emotion, aesthetics, travel, commute, family, or lifestyle attitude drives the content; the product is weakly exposed. Brand proposition/Campaign belongs to BRANDING; product decision information belongs to SEEDING.
3. **探店/服务体验** - Store, environment, service, and experience are the main line without strong promotion. Brand events belong to BRANDING; deals, discounts, or redemption mechanisms belong to OFFER.

## BRANDING

1. **品牌广告/Campaign** - Brand tone, visual assets, proposition, festival theme, or Campaign concept is primary; detailed product education is not. Product efficacy/use/effect as the main line belongs to SEEDING; promotion as the main line belongs to OFFER.
2. **代言人/IP/联名** - Celebrity, IP, crossover, or brand-person asset is the core viewing point. A creator's personal product review belongs to SEEDING.
3. **品牌故事/科技/事件** - Brand history, R&D, values, launch, pop-up, or event is the main line. Ingredient/efficacy information that drives purchase belongs to SEEDING; new-product first tests also belong to SEEDING.

## OFFER

1. **优惠促销/直接售卖** - Price, discount, coupon, threshold discount, gift, time limit, stock, or purchase instruction drives immediate conversion. Usually require at least two strong signals among price, discount, urgency, cart, and CTA, or require the promotion mechanism to be the main line. A single weak CTA at the end does not trigger OFFER.
2. **直播预告** - Directs users to a live room at a specified time and highlights products or benefits. Require at least two among time, live room, product, benefit, and action instruction.
3. **直播切片/成交高光** - Derived from a live stream and retains price mechanics, benefits, order instructions, or conversion language. A live knowledge clip without conversion mechanics is classified by its content main line.

## OTHERS

1. **非相关内容** - Unrelated to target brands, products, consumer research, or the designated industry. Do not use when recognizable product decision content exists.
